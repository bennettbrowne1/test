The Science

How It Works

Afterimages arise when photoreceptors fatigue from sustained stimulation. Flash Afterimage engineers this effect deliberately.

01

Flash

A high-luminance inverted version of your asset is displayed for 2–4 seconds, saturating cone cells.

02

Fatigue

Cones sensitive to the stimulus colours become temporarily desensitised, creating a neural imbalance.

03

Afterimage

When the stimulus is removed, opposing cones dominate — projecting a vivid complementary ghost image.

04

Memory

The unusual perceptual experience cements the brand in episodic memory, boosting aided recall by up to 3×.
